river

MediumSound-interactive experimental film
Year2024
Technologyp5.js

A river is not only a physical body of water, but also a carrier full of symbolic meaning. It carries the echoes of history and the footprints of time, and perhaps it is always more than just "flowing water"; it is a window for us to think about our existence, memory and future. Every time we gaze at a river, we are seeing images of ourselves superimposed on each other.
