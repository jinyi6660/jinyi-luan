Lighthouse

MediumProduct design, UI/UX
Year2024
TypeSmart companion device concept

Lighthouse is an integrated service combining a mobile app, volunteer network and connected home device to support families searching for missing children. Through reported leads, communication tools, psychological support and ambient information delivery, it aims to make the long-term search process more connected and emotionally supportive.
