Verdant Her

MediumGenerative AI, ALife ecosystem
Year2024
TechnologyBlender
Collaboratorswith Huiyuan Meng, Xintong Yang
Project poster · Group collaboration, Group 2.4

A virtual ecosystem exploring the intersection of ALife (artificial life), generative AI and ecofeminism — using a flower's pistil and stamen structures as metaphor, with real gender-gap data (maternal mortality, adolescent birth rate, female labour participation, and more) driving the generation and evolution of its forms.

The work hopes to integrate the vitality of plants and women, and express the vitality of women's power.
