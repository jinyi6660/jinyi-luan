As an artist

Working as an artist has taught me to pay closer attention to my relationship with the world. I observe social changes, question how they shape our emotions and everyday experiences, and translate my own perceptions into artistic forms. Rather than simply providing answers, I use art to create space for resonance, reflection, and new ways of engaging with constantly evolving social and technological questions.
