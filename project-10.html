What the Machine Guesses

MediumExperimental film
Year2026
TechnologyMachine Learning

The work focuses on the mix of algorithm bias, emotion simplification, identity technology, and human embodied feelings. It tries to break the boundary between human perception and machine understanding, and shows both the connection and conflict between machines and human emotions in an algorithmic society. It also shows how technology can break down and reshape human emotional experience, creating new forms of existence.
