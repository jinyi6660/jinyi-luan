About

I am an interdisciplinary artist and designer working across computational art, visual design, and interactive experiences.

My practice is driven by an interest in how we perceive and experience existence within an increasingly data-driven world. I observe shifting relationships between humans, technology, other species, and their surrounding environments, with particular attention to embodied experience, memory, emotion, anxiety, and the everyday.

Rather than treating data and AI simply as technologies, I use them as creative methods to make experiences that are often invisible, intangible, or difficult to articulate perceptible again.
