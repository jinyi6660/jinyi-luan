the tree looks back

MediumInteractive installation
Year2025
TechnologyTouchDesigner, motor servo, distance sensor, clay, 3D printing, webcam

This is an interactive installation: an anthropomorphised tree bearing deep time and possessing its own thoughts, situated within a space of blurred boundaries. It serves as a metaphor for our performative pseudo-era — where distinctions between boundaries and intimacy lose their clear definition.

The tree observes, records, and reacts to human presence, thereby critiquing surveillance and performativity while exploring the relationship between watching and being watched.
