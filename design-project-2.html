Life Assistant

MediumProduct design, UI/UX
Year2023
TypeCompanion device concept for elderly care

Life Assistant is an accessible service and product designed for people with limited literacy. By combining recognisable visual icons, a simplified 3D interface and voice guidance, it helps users complete everyday tasks without relying on written instructions.
